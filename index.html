ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Big ideas.
At a different scale.

This interactive city explores a possible digital showcase for KMP Model Makers. Select a coloured building to discover a model study, or use Walk and Fly through to explore the city.

All six models are original, procedural illustrations. They are not KMP projects. No actual portfolio, business figures or contact details are represented.

This prototype has no accounts, payments, tracking or data collection.
